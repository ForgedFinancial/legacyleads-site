Daniel Ruh - licensed life-insurance agent

Insurance work with a real person behind it.

Daniel built Legacy Leads for working families who want straight answers before they make an important decision. The practice is trucker-first because people who keep the country moving deserve an agent who works around real schedules.

Why this practice exists

Honest work is part of the legacy.

Daniel documents his own debt-payoff journey as the Road to Freedom. It is not a promise about anyone else's finances. It is the personal throughline behind how he works: face the numbers, tell the truth, and make the next responsible decision.

That same standard belongs in an insurance conversation. Daniel listens first, explains the real trade-offs, and gives families room to decide. If a policy is not right for you, he will say so.

Who Daniel helps

Working families first.

Life does not happen on an office schedule. Legacy Leads is built to meet people where they are, keep the conversation clear, and focus on the people the coverage is meant to protect.

Truck drivers

Conversations planned around the road, home time, and the practical needs of a family whose income depends on keeping moving.

Working families

Plain-language help for income replacement, a mortgage, final bills, and other responsibilities that do not disappear when life changes.

People who want clarity

No fear tactics and no rush. Just the options, the trade-offs, and enough room to make your own decision.
