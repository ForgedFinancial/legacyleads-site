In-person life-insurance sales - Schaumburg, IL

Be led well. Build a lasting practice.

Daniel Ruh is interviewing candidates for an in-person life-insurance sales role at our Schaumburg, IL office. This is commission-only, 1099 independent-contractor work. There is no salary, base, or benefits. Candidates must be able to commute to Schaumburg in the Chicago northwest suburbs and work on-site with the team.

The work

Direct, useful, customer-facing.

The work is straightforward: call people who requested information about life insurance, learn their needs, explain available coverage, and help qualified families apply.

You are paid when a family gets covered, and not before. Daniel shows the structure in writing at onboarding - your contract level, how advances work, and what a chargeback is.

The structure, in writing

Know the arrangement before you commit.

This page describes the contractor structure, costs, and support. It does not make an earnings claim.

Honest startup cost

Plan for the real first-90-days expense.

Your realistic first-90-days out-of-pocket: $300-600 state licensing now, roughly $300-600 E&O when you contract, $25-50 starter leads - up to about $1,250 total, self-funded. If that's a wall, this seat isn't right yet.

For an unlicensed candidate, the license is a 20-60 hour course plus a state exam.

If your license is active, your out-of-pocket is just E&O at contracting plus any starter leads you choose to self-fund; the full roughly $1,250 first-90-days figure only applies to someone entering unlicensed.

Straight talk

Who this is not for

Straight talk on who this is not for: if you need income inside 30 days, cannot self-fund the roughly $1,250 start, or will not run a proven script before rewriting it, this seat is not right yet.

This is also not the right seat if you cannot commute to Schaumburg, cannot protect consistent weekly hours, or do not want to work alongside the team in the office.

Before course-purchase advice

Carrier appointment comes first.

Before anyone advises a course purchase, Daniel discusses factors that may affect carrier appointment, including criminal history, bankruptcy or severe credit events, prior insurance-agent debt, carrier balances, or a Vector One listing. Carrier appointment is uncertain until any flagged issue is resolved.

How much will I make?

Daniel will not quote an income number. At onboarding, he shows the commission structure in writing - contract level, how advances work, and what a chargeback is. We promise the system, not a paycheck.
